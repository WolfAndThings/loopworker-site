What does the free snapshot include?

A short, evidence-backed observation about your firm's public marketing or competitive environment, including a relevant source and a practical next step. It is a sample of the research process, not a full market analysis.

What's included in the $750 Market Read?

A focused review of your firm's public-facing marketing and up to 3 relevant competitors, the meaningful opportunities, prioritized recommendations, and a suggested content direction. It is a focused first engagement, not a comprehensive audit of every channel or competitor.

What's the difference between the monthly plans?

Both plans include ongoing competitive research, a monthly content plan, and eight ready-to-film scripts. The $8,999 plan also includes editing up to eight finished videos from footage your team supplies.

Can we change the number of videos?

Yes. Eight videos is the standard monthly production scope. A different quantity or production complexity can be quoted separately and documented before work starts.

How does this differ from a traditional marketing agency?

LoopWorker focuses on understanding the market before deciding what to make. It provides the research, recommendations, and creative direction your team can use, with optional editing and production.

Can LoopWorker work with our existing marketing team?

Yes. The service is designed to complement an existing internal marketing team or agency.

Do you need access to our accounts?

No account access is required for the public-data snapshot. Deeper performance analysis may benefit from reports supplied by your team, but your firm retains account ownership and control.

Who films the videos?

Your firm's attorneys, partners, or designated spokespeople. LoopWorker provides scripts and recording guidance. Your team records and uploads the footage.

Does LoopWorker post the content?

No. Your team retains control of its accounts and handles publishing.

Are revisions included?

Yes. Intelligence + Content includes one consolidated revision round on the monthly content package. Intelligence + Production also includes up to two consolidated revision rounds for each finished video, within the agreed scope.

Can you guarantee new clients or leads?

No. LoopWorker helps improve marketing decisions and content direction using observable evidence. It does not guarantee rankings, inquiries, signed matters, or revenue.

How are legal advertising rules handled?

Your firm remains responsible for reviewing and approving legal claims, testimonials, attorney statements, and other materials subject to applicable professional advertising requirements.
