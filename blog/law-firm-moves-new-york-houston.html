Market moves: New York leads, Houston gets a new office

Public law news reported 20 moves from October 19 to October 25, 2026. New York had the most, and Ashurst Perkins Coie opened in Houston.

October 26, 2026 · Alex Lamb

Quick answer

New York led the window with 4 of 20 reported moves, all of them partners or teams taken from other law firms by Weil Gotshal & Manges, Sullivan & Cromwell, Allen Matkins Leck Gamble Mallory & Natsis and Blank Rome. Houston was the other story, with Ashurst Perkins Coie opening an office there and Kirkland & Ellis and Nelson Mullins Riley & Scarborough adding lawyers in the city.

Key takeaways
  • Public law news reported 20 moves: 14 laterals, 4 group moves, 2 office openings and 0 mergers.
  • New York had the most moves, with 4, and each came from another law firm.
  • Ashurst Perkins Coie opened in Houston, and The Lawyer reported it launched with lawyers from Bracewell.

  • Kirkland & Ellis and Nelson Mullins Riley & Scarborough each gained and lost a lawyer.

New York led the window

Public law news reported 20 moves from October 19 to October 25, 2026. That breaks into 14 laterals, 4 group moves, 2 office openings and 0 mergers. New York had the most, with 4.

Each New York arrival came from another law firm. Weil Gotshal & Manges hired an antitrust partner from Paul Weiss Rifkind Wharton & Garrison. Sullivan & Cromwell added a tax lawyer from Kirkland & Ellis as partner. Allen Matkins Leck Gamble Mallory & Natsis hired Edward E. Filusch, a commercial litigator, from Kasowitz.

Blank Rome made the group move. It added Christopher Doyle and Marvin Goldstein, a commodities and finance team, from Hogan Lovells Cadwalader. Global Legal Post reported it.

If your firm competes in New York antitrust, tax, commercial litigation or commodities finance, a rival now has a new partner to put in front of your clients.

Houston: a new office and Texas hires

Ashurst Perkins Coie opened an office in Houston. Both of its counted moves are reports of that launch, from LawFuel and The Lawyer. The Lawyer reported the office launched with lawyers from Bracewell.

Other firms added in Houston too. Kirkland & Ellis took a longtime Susman Godfrey litigator in Houston and Austin. Nelson Mullins Riley & Scarborough added 3 tax and litigation lawyers in Atlanta and Houston.

Dallas had 2 moves. Munsch Hardt Kopf & Harr PC added an M&A lawyer from Dorsey & Whitney. Hunton Andrews Kurth hired a commercial litigator from Husch Blackwell.

For a Houston firm, a new office brings a new name into pitches, rankings submissions and local press. The launch announcement is where that entrant introduces itself.

Philadelphia and the rest of the map

Troutman Pepper Locke is tied with Ashurst Perkins Coie as the top gaining firm, with 2. Both entries describe an insurance litigation team joining in Philadelphia. Law360 Pennsylvania reported 3 litigators came from Faegre Drinker Biddle & Reath.

Washington, DC had 2 moves. Freshfields hired a Life Sciences lawyer from Gibson Dunn & Crutcher. Baker Donelson Bearman Caldwell & Berkowitz PC hired a former White House information director from Nelson Mullins Riley & Scarborough.

San Francisco had 2. Ogletree Deakins Nash Smoak & Stewart PC hired a privacy, cybersecurity and artificial intelligence counsel from Fisher Phillips. DLA Piper hired a product liability partner from Shook Hardy & Bacon.

The rest were single entries on the map. Haber Law added a real estate partner from Greenspoon Marder in Miami. Norris McLaughlin PA added a lawyer from Eckert Seamans Cherin & Mellott in Allentown. Brownstein Hyatt Farber Schreck added a government relations lawyer from the U.S. Department of Agriculture in D.C. Bressler Amery & Ross PC added an employment lawyer from Standard Chartered Bank in Florham Park.

Firms on both sides of the ledger

Kirkland & Ellis appears on both lists. It lost a tax lawyer to Sullivan & Cromwell in New York and gained a Susman Godfrey litigator in Texas.

Nelson Mullins Riley & Scarborough also appears on both lists. It lost a lawyer to Baker Donelson Bearman Caldwell & Berkowitz PC in Washington, DC and added 3 lawyers in Atlanta and Houston.

That matters for how each firm tells the story. A departure and an arrival in the same window produce 2 announcements, and only the arrival is the firm's to make.

What it means for your next decision

A lateral arrival is the moment a firm's marketing team has the most to announce: a new name, a practice to point to, a city to claim. The hiring firm owns that moment. The firm that lost the lawyer does not.

If you compete in New York, Houston or Philadelphia litigation, the firms named here have something new to say this month. Your own clients may hear it first from them.
