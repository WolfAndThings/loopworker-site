Market moves, October 12 to October 18, 2026: Washington, DC is where the moves clustered

Public law news reported 10 moves this week. Washington, DC was the only city with 2, both of them lateral hires.

October 19, 2026 · Alex Lamb

Quick answer

Public law news reported 10 moves in October 12 to October 18, 2026, and Washington, DC was the only city with more than 1. Wilson Sonsini Goodrich & Rosati PC hired a national security lead from Baker Botts there, and Foley Hoag hired a mergers and acquisitions and private equity lawyer from Miles & Stockbridge PC.

Key takeaways
  • Public law news reported 10 moves this week: 7 laterals, 2 group moves, 1 office opening and 0 mergers.
  • Washington, DC had 2 moves, and every other city on the list had 1.
  • Wilson Sonsini Goodrich & Rosati PC and Foley Hoag each added a lateral in Washington, DC.
  • No firm appears more than once on either the gaining or the losing side.
  • Energy hires landed in Houston and Austin, and litigation hires landed in Boston, Pensacola and Pittsburgh.

Washington, DC was the only city with 2 moves

Public law news reported 10 moves in October 12 to October 18, 2026. Washington, DC was the only metro with more than 1. Every other city on the list had 1.

Both Washington, DC moves were laterals. Wilson Sonsini Goodrich & Rosati PC hired the national security and trade risk mitigation lead from Baker Botts. Foley Hoag hired a mergers and acquisitions and private equity lawyer from Miles & Stockbridge PC.

The 2 practices are different. What they share is the city. If your firm competes in Washington, DC, this is the week a rival got something new to say.

What it means if you compete in Washington, DC

If your firm sells national security and trade risk mitigation work in Washington, DC, Wilson Sonsini Goodrich & Rosati PC now has a fresh name in that practice. Law360 Legal Industry reported the hire. Your clients and prospects in that practice may have read it.

The same goes for mergers and acquisitions and private equity. Foley Hoag's hire ran in Law360 Private Equity.

A lateral arrival is the moment a firm's marketing team has the most to announce. The gaining firm has a new bio, a new voice and a reason to contact the market. If you compete with Wilson Sonsini Goodrich & Rosati PC or Foley Hoag in these practices, your next decision is whether your own people in that practice are visible this month.

For Baker Botts and Miles & Stockbridge PC, the news runs the other way. Each now has a departure on the public record in Washington, DC.

Energy and litigation, spread across cities

Energy shows up in Houston and Austin. Dykema Gossett added an energy lawyer and a trial lawyer in Houston, reported as a group move. Troutman Pepper Locke added an energy regulatory lawyer in Austin, coming from an in-house role at Lower Colorado River Authority. Both ran in Law360 Energy.

Litigation moves landed in different cities. Womble added a financial litigation team from K&L Gates in Boston, reported by Global Legal Post. GrayRobinson PA hired a litigator from Clark Partington in Pensacola. WH Burkley added a commercial litigation and election law lawyer from Leech Tishman in Pittsburgh.

The rest of the week: Greenberg Traurig hired a private funds lawyer from Sidley Austin in Chicago. Marshall Dennehey added a casualty lawyer from Quintairos Prieto Wood & Boyer PA in Orlando. Mandelbaum Barrett PC opened an office in Lakewood.

The firms on each side

No firm appears more than once on the gaining side. No firm appears more than once on the losing side. Each firm named this week has 1 move.

Womble's move is the only group move with a source firm named in the reporting. Dykema Gossett's group move in Houston names no source firm.

That matters for how you read your own market. If a competitor appears on this list, it appears for a single hire or office. The signal is the practice and the city, not a firm on a hiring run.
